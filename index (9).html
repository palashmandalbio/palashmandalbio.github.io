About

Palash Mandal

Professor, Biological Sciences

PDPIAS, CHARUSAT University · Changa, Gujarat

I am a Professor in the Department of Biological Sciences at P. D. Patel Institute of Applied Sciences, CHARUSAT University. I am honored to be recognized among the World's Top 5% Scientists in the 2025 SciRank Global Registry.

My research focuses on TLR4 signaling, oxidative stress and inflammation in alcoholic and non-alcoholic fatty liver diseases (ALD/MASLD), gut microbiota in liver disease, innate immunity, cytokines, and biomarkers of apoptosis. I completed my postdoctoral and research associate training at the Cleveland Clinic, USA (2003–2010).

I serve as Associate Editor for Frontiers in Molecular Biosciences and Frontiers in Gastroenterology, and as Academic Editor for Scientific Reports, PLOS ONE, Mediators of Inflammation, and several other leading journals.
